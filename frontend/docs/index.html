Introduction

Request capabilities, fund their creation, and review builder proposals.

UNMET is an onchain demand market for missing AI-agent capabilities. Requesters describe what they need and commit funds. Other supporters can add funding. Builders propose a service, and supporters decide whether it earns a payout.

Why UNMET?

A service catalog shows what already exists. UNMET makes unmet needs visible, together with committed funding, expected calls, and price limits. Builders can compare demand before choosing what to build.

How UNMET works

  1. A requester describes a capability and creates a funded demand.
  2. Supporters add commitments while the demand is open.
  3. A builder submits a service URL and evidence.
  4. Supporters approve or reject it with commitment-weighted votes.
  5. Once quorum is reached, anyone can finalize. Only approver funds settle; eligible remaining funds can be refunded.

Three separate economic flows

  • Demand funding finances service creation through escrow.
  • Intelligence payments buy a ranked demand query.
  • Service usage is paid separately to a provider. A demand contribution does not grant API credits or included calls.

Supporter approval and fulfillment do not prove service quality or future availability. The escrow contract has not been independently audited. Read review and fulfillment before committing funds.
