For requesters

Describe a missing capability, fund a demand, or support an existing request.

Before you start

Use a compatible wallet on X Layer Testnet 1952. You need gas and the configured Testnet escrow token. Mainnet tokens used for intelligence payments are a separate payment rail.

Find or create a demand

  1. Open Market and check existing requests for your capability.
  2. To join a request, open its Escrow & Support tab and choose Support This Request.
  3. For a new need, open Create Demand. Describe the capability, requirements, price ceiling, expected calls, funding, and deadline.
  4. Review the terms and intended wallet. Approve the required token allowance if requested, then confirm the funding transaction.

See creating and funding demand for field requirements.

After funding

Funding remains in escrow. When a builder proposes a service, inspect its endpoint and evidence before voting. Your pre-submission commitment determines your voting weight.

Expected calls express intended demand, not purchased usage. Funding does not guarantee that a builder will deliver or that supporters will approve a proposal.

Check the outcome

Wait for a successful transaction receipt and updated demand state. If the outcome is pending or unknown, inspect the existing transaction before submitting again. An eligible refund requires a separate withdrawal transaction.

Continue with review and fulfillment or refunds.
