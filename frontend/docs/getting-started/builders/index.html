For builders

Find funded demand and submit a service for supporter review.

Choose a demand

Browse Market for requirements you can meet. Compare current escrow, specifications, maximum unit price, expected calls, and deadline. Paid Demand Intelligence can help compare opportunities.

Escrow is not guaranteed earnings. Expected calls are a demand signal, not an order for future usage.

Build and propose

  1. Choose an open demand before its deadline.
  2. Build and host a service that meets the request.
  3. Connect the wallet that should be recorded as the builder.
  4. Open Builder Proposal, provide the HTTPS service URL and evidence, and select Submit Service Proposal.

The current flow does not require prior builder registration. Automatic x402 validation and signed attestations are proposed, not part of current proposal acceptance.

Review and payment

Supporters approve or reject your proposal. Reaching quorum allows finalization; it does not automatically transfer funds. The builder receives only explicitly approved funds less the configured protocol fee.

A demand contribution does not prepay service calls. Any later provider usage is a separate arrangement. UNMET does not currently provide a fulfilled-service registry or capability resolver.
