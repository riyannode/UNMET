Refunds

Withdraw your own remaining commitment when the contract makes it eligible.

Refund eligibility

Demand conditionCan you refund?
Open, deadline still activeNo.
Deadline passed without a proposalYes, for your remaining commitment.
Submitted, below quorumAfter the demand deadline, once review ends or rejection makes quorum impossible.
Submitted, quorum reachedNo. Anyone can finalize; approval is not a failed review.
FulfilledYes if you did not approve the settled proposal and still have a commitment.

Withdraw a refund

Connect the wallet that funded the demand, open Escrow & Support, and use Refund when eligible. Each supporter withdraws their own funds; there is no automatic bulk refund. Gas is required for the transaction.

Wait for a successful receipt and updated commitment. An RPC failure after broadcasting is not permission to withdraw again blindly.

After a refund

Your remaining commitment and expected calls for that position are cleared. Current escrow falls, but aggregate supporting-wallet and expected-call figures remain historical demand signals.

A non-fulfilled demand becomes CLOSED when its last refundable balance is withdrawn. A fulfilled demand stays FULFILLED, including after its final non-approver refund.

Before the deadline, an unsuccessful proposal may instead be reopened under its current review conditions.
