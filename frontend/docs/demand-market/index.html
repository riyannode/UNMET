Creating & funding demand

Make a capability request concrete with requirements, a deadline, and an escrow commitment.

Demand terms

TermRequirement
CapabilityLowercase letters and digits separated by single hyphens; at most 64 bytes.
SpecificationDescribe inputs, expected outputs, and acceptance needs. Nonempty; at most 2,048 bytes.
Maximum unit pricePositive service-price ceiling, entered with up to six decimal places.
Expected callsPositive integer estimating intended usage; not purchased calls.
DeadlineAt least one hour ahead at creation. It cannot be extended after creation.
Initial commitmentMeet the configured minimum using the market's six-decimal escrow token.

The contract rejects an exact active duplicate with the same capability and specification. It does not match semantically similar requests.

Create a demand

Open Create Demand, complete the guided questions, review all six terms, then choose Create & Fund Demand. The wallet may request a token allowance transaction before funding. Check the network, allowance, and amount before signing.

Support a demand

Open a demand's Escrow & Support tab. Enter a commitment meeting the configured minimum and positive expected calls. Funding is accepted only while the demand is open and before its deadline.

No additional support is accepted during proposal review. Follow builder proposals to understand what happens next.

What your commitment means

Escrow finances service creation. It does not buy intelligence, reserve service capacity, or include future API calls. Supporting-wallet counts and aggregate expected calls are historical signals; later refunds do not reduce those aggregate figures.

Keep the existing transaction hash if an outcome is unknown. Confirm its receipt and demand state before attempting another funding action.
