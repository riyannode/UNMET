Review & fulfillment

Supporters decide with commitment-weighted votes. Only approved commitments can settle.

Approve or reject

Open Builder Proposal and inspect the service and evidence. Each supporter's weight is their commitment before submission. A supporter may approve or reject once per proposal, never both. Voting closes at the review end time.

The approval threshold is rounded up from the fixed escrow snapshot. Refunds cannot shrink that threshold. Supporting wallets are not verified independent people.

Finalize at quorum

Once approval reaches the fixed threshold, anyone may choose Finalize Payout. The full review period need not elapse. Reaching quorum is not itself settlement; a successful finalization transaction is required.

The configured fee, quorum, and review period are contract values. Initial release defaults are 2%, 60%, and 24 hours respectively; these defaults are not a fresh deployment readback.

Selective settlement

Only explicit approver commitments settle. The builder receives that amount minus the protocol fee. Non-approver funds remain eligible for individual refunds.

Illustrative example with a 2% fee: a 100-token review snapshot has 60 tokens approved. Finalization sends 58.8 to the builder and 1.2 to treasury. The other 40 remain refundable to eligible non-approvers.

Rejection and reopening

If rejection makes quorum mathematically impossible, anyone can reopen before the demand deadline. Otherwise, reopening requires the review to end below quorum while the deadline is still active. A reopened demand keeps its original deadline and clears proposal-specific votes.

Read the outcome correctly

READY means quorum has been reached; FULFILLED means escrow settlement completed. Neither proves independent service correctness or future endpoint availability.

If the demand cannot proceed, check refund eligibility.
